# MAPLE: Multi-Alpha Position-aware Listwise Ensembling

**Source**: arxiv:2607.24131v1 (July 2026)

## Overview
Backbone-agnostic framework for efficient and diverse multi-alpha portfolio construction.

## Problem Addressed
- Deep learning typically produces single alpha per stock
- Multi-model ensembles are expensive and don't control correlation
- Need for diverse, low-correlated alpha signals

## MAPLE Architecture

### Unified Prediction Head
- Intra-stock path: individual stock features
- Inter-stock attention: cross-stock relationships
- Capacity scaling per alpha

### Loss Design
1. **Extreme-Rank Weighted Listwise Loss**: Focuses on extreme-rank positions relevant to portfolio
2. **Diversity Regularizer**: Explicitly penalizes pairwise alpha correlation

### Capacity Scaling
- Per-alpha capacity increases with number of alphas
- Prevents degradation in naive multi-alpha designs

## Results

### Performance
- Best average Sharpe and Calmar ratios among 9 baselines
- 10-23% Sharpe gains across 5 backbones
- 17-43% Calmar gains
- 55x fewer parameters than DHMoE
- 2.5x faster training

### Markets Tested
- US equities
- China CSI500
- Japan NI225
- EuroStoxx50

### Key Insights
- Unified head reduces inter-alpha correlation before diversity loss
- Extreme-rank weighting lets diversity improve ranking quality
- Capacity scaling sustains balance at scale

## Implications for Quant Trading
- Principled loss design > architectural complexity
- Efficient single-model multi-alpha generation
- Diversity regularization is critical for robust ensembles
