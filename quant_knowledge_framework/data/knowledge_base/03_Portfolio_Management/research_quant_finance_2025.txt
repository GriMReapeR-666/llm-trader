# Advances in Quantitative Finance 2025: From Models to Systems

**Source**: magazine.thalesians.com (December 2025)

## Overview
Annual review of quantitative finance research trends in 2025.

## Key Themes

### 1. System-Level View
- Markets as interacting, data-rich, path-dependent systems
- Market generators and particle simulations
- Scenario-based methods for tail events
- Square-root impact law as universal pattern

### 2. AI Embedded, Not Idolized
- ML, generative models, RL used selectively
- Extends rather than replaces classical methods
- Markowitz enhanced with ML
- RL for genuinely sequential decisions

### 3. Risk and Execution as Organizing Principles
- Dynamic, time-consistent risk
- Execution-aware portfolio choice
- Robust pricing under model uncertainty
- Liquidity and microstructure built into strategies

## Notable Research Areas

### Data-Driven Hedging with Generative Models
- Synthetic data for portfolio construction
- Scenario generation for stress testing

### Reinforcement Learning for Portfolio Management
- Adaptive trading strategies
- Dynamic portfolio allocation
- Challenges: training difficulty, validation sensitivity

### Optimal Liquidation with Signals
- Exact formulas for best trading strategy
- Price impact and risk considerations
- General propagator case solutions

### Dynamic Portfolio Choice with Transaction Costs
- Intertemporal hedging
- Execution risk integration
- Target portfolio preparation for future opportunities

### FX Market Making with Internal Liquidity
- Internal liquidity pool management
- Multi-objective optimization (risk, profit, speed)
- Predictable execution behavior for other LPs

### Enhancing Markowitz with ML
- Traditional mean-variance + ML enhancements
- Robustness in modern markets
