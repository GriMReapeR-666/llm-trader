## Key concepts
Normal Distribution MLE Estimators: 
$$
\hat\mu = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} x_t \\\qquad \hat\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}{(x_t - \hat\mu)^2}}
$$


Exponential Distribution MLE Estimators: 
$$\hat\lambda = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{T} x_t}{T}$$
