09.2 Triage: deterministic first, model-assisted second

data/news_triage.py is where a headline becomes a tradeable or ignorable object:

  • dedupe_key_for(url, title) (~254) gives each article a stable identity so the same story cannot be counted twice across feeds.
  • _deterministic_r1 (~198) and the deterministic label family (_deterministic_d5a ~518, _deterministic_d9 ~536, _deterministic_labels ~559) classify an article without a model.
  • _resolve_action(d0_1, d0_2, d0_3, policy_allowed) (~616) maps labels to an action, and policy_allowed can suppress the action regardless of label.
  • _get_laya() (~459) optionally loads the Laya classifier; _pick_better(qid, raw_answer, det_label, laya_probs) (~642) chooses between the deterministic label and the model's probabilities, and triage_article/triage_articles (~698, ~841) produce the final verdicts.
  • TriageGate.apply(rows) (~320) and is_rejected(article) (~329) mark rejected items; summarize_cycle_evidence() (~341) records the evidence used in the cycle's decision.
Why this matters for trading. The news gate in §04.2 reads these verdicts. Because a deterministic classifier exists and can overrule the model, a hallucinating classifier degrades news handling to "conservative", not to "reactive".
