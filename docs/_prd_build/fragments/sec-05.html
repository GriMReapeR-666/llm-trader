05 05. The AI Decision & Permission Path

This section explains "how the AI trades" — and why the answer is: it does not. The AI emits a proposal; a deterministic chain decides. §02.5 listed the vetoes; here we follow one proposal from generation to order.

05.3 From decision to TradeProposal

_validate_proposal (~1960) turns a decision plus market context into the immutable TradeProposal (core/trading_safety.py ~40) and immediately calls validator.validate(proposal, market). The proposal carries strategy, symbol, side, entry, stop loss, take profit, confidence, a feature dict, and a setup_key/setup_id used for deduplication and lifecycle tracking (states CANDIDATE → VALIDATED → ACCEPTED | MISSED | INVALIDATED | REJECTED | UNRESOLVED → CLOSED, core/trading_safety.py ~382–407). Magic numbers distinguish strategy families: 460001 swing, 460002 scalp.

Approved and rejected proposals share the same journal path, which is why rejected ideas stay analysable without being re-traded.

05.5 Position sizing: a stack of reduction-only multipliers

RiskManager.calc_position_size (core/risk_manager.py ~861) is the only place a volume number is produced. Its shape: a 1R risk budget derived from account equity and stop distance, multiplied by a chain of scalars, each of which can only reduce (or zero) the size.

volume ← f( equity × risk_per_trade , stop_distance , tick_value ) hard rejects first (return 0.0): permanent_halt | equity_floor | daily_drawdown | consecutive_loss_breaker | max_drawdown portfolio heat: heat_now + 1R > max_portfolio_heat_r → 0 entry == sl → 0 multiplicative ladder: size_mult = f(confidence) <0.60 → 0 ; <0.75 → 0.5 ; else 1.0 size_mult *= circuit_multiplier weekly circuit step-down size_mult *= dd_multiplier drawdown ladder (0 at ≥20% DD) size_mult *= streak_multiplier anti-martingale loss streak size_mult *= kelly_multiplier quarter-Kelly, reduction-only size_mult *= spmc_multiplier SPMC regime: 1.0 good / 0.25 bad size_mult *= opp_multiplier opportunity-cost hurdle; 0 → reject retail-quant overlay (core/retail_quant_models.py): CUSUM breach (k=0.5, h=4.0, ≥20 R-samples) → ×0.5 Bayesian win-rate shrinkage (last 50) → × p_shrink ≤ 1 Correlation-breakdown heat (window 60, k=2.5)→ × mult ≤ 1 volatility & model overlays: ATR targeting: × clamp(atr_target / atr, 0.5, 2.0) Alpha sizing: × compute_alpha_sizing(composite, direction, IC, confidence) Alpha risk: × alpha_risk_profile.sizing_multiplier Kronos: × kronos_risk_adjustment_factor (0–1) research health gates (can zero the trade): walk_forward_status = DEAD → 0 DECAYING → ×0.5 WFE (≥2 folds): mean_test_sharpe ≤ 0 → 0 ; WFE < 0.5 → × max(0.5, WFE) overfit_risk_level = CRITICAL → 0 HIGH → ×0.25 data_drift_status = DRIFT → ×0.5 CVaR95 > 2R (≥15 samples) → × clamp(2 / CVaR_r, 0.20, 1.0)
Why the design matters. Every model-derived input (Kronos, alpha composite, walk-forward state, overfit level, drift) can only shrink a position. A hallucinated or over-fit model cannot inflate size: the LLM's own confidence field is capped at 1.0, and any zero is a hard reject.

05.6 The portfolio arbiter: the second opinion on aggregate risk

05.7 The last reductions before the broker: P8 cap, plan, margin

  • P8 allocation cap (apply_portfolio_allocation_cap ~2685) may only reduce volume. If it reduces to zero, the cycle journals zero_volume_rejected and HOLDs.
  • Immutable execution plan (_build_execution_plan ~2830) validates the quote (validate_quote(max_tick_age_ms, max_spread), core/execution_planning.py ~127) and the pending-entry geometry (build_pending_entry ~73). A ValueError becomes a HOLD plus a lifecycle REJECTED event — the plan is never "fixed up" and sent anyway.
  • Margin safety (core/margin_safety.py) — in live mode assess_from_mt5() is used; otherwise assess() with the plan's own snapshot. A refusal lists its reasons in the HOLD.
