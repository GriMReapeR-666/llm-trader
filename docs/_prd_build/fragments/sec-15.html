15.1 The discovery loop and its sandbox

AlphaDiscoveryEngine (core/alpha_discovery.py ~505) searches for alphas under constraints that matter more than the search itself:

GuardImplementationPurpose
Expression allow-list_ALLOWED_NAMES (~54)Only sanctioned operators/functions may appear in a candidate
Column allow-list_ALLOWED_COLUMNS = {"open","high","low","close","volume","returns"} (~62)No leakage from future columns, labels or exogenous series
Safe evaluationSafeAlphaEvaluator (~65)Evaluates candidate ASTs without arbitrary execution
Hermetic runsSealedSandboxEvaluator (~281)Backtests run sealed; a candidate cannot reach the network, disk or broker
Originality_normalize_ast / _ast_similarity / check_originality (~197–259)Rejects near-duplicates of already-registered alphas
Experience memoryExperienceMemory (~413), AlphaExperienceMemory (~440, store-backed)Remembers what was tried so the loop does not rediscover failures

Bootstrapping also runs a fast genetic pass: GAEngine(GAConfig(population_size=15, generations=1, min_ic_threshold=0.005)) over 1,000 H1 bars, logging evaluated/significant counts (mt5_trader.py ~4134–4153).

15.2 The factor library

core/alpha_factors.py implements the operator vocabulary (ts_rank ~28, ts_argmax/argmin ~39/~44, ts_min/max ~49/~54, delta ~59, delay ~64, rank ~69, scale ~78, decay_linear ~86, signed_power ~97, log_safe ~102) and a numbered alpha family (alpha_001 … alpha_060) plus a dedicated persistence family:

persistence family (time-series momentum with vol normalisation) _tsmom_z(df, horizon_bars, vol_window?) = z-score of cumulative return over horizon alpha_persistence_5d / 10d / 20d / 40d = fixed-horizon variants alpha_persistence_ma_gap = price vs moving-average gap alpha_persistence_sign_agreement = agreement of sign across lookbacks usage ic_t = corr( alpha_t , forward_return_{t+h} ) # information coefficient composite = Σ w_i · zscore( alpha_i ) # alpha_composite in sizing

Orthogonalisation and redundancy control live in core/signal_orthogonalizer.py and core/causal_feature_filter.py; capacity limits in core/alpha_capacity.py; sizing in core/alpha_sizing.py (§05.5).

15.3 Decay, rotation and the live hand-off

ComponentModuleResponsibility
Decay monitorcore/alpha_decay_monitor.py + alpha_decay_state.jsonTracks IC decay and demotes alphas that stop working
Rotationcore/alpha_rotation.py + alpha_rotation_state.jsonReplaces dead members of the active alpha set
Ensemblecore/alpha_ensemble.pyCombines member alphas into a composite with quality weighting
Discovery statealpha_discovery_state.jsonLoop progress and experience memory persistence
Live hand-off_apply_alpha_mining_enrichment(toon) (intelligence/llm_orchestrator.py ~4399)Injects alpha features/IC into the decision payload
Zero live authority. A mined alpha can enrich the payload and reduce or zero a position (through the alpha-risk and walk-forward multipliers), but it can never open a trade. Only a validated LLM proposal does that. alpha_discovery_state.json and alpha_rotation_state.json are advisory state, not permissions.
