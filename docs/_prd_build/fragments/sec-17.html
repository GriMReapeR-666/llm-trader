17 17. ML / RL Stack: Kronos, PPO, Meta-RL, Bandits & Supervised Models

One sentence frames this whole section: no model here can open, close, resize or re-target a position on its own. Every output is either an advisory input to the prompt (§05.1) or a reduction-only multiplier (§05.5). Updates are additionally gated by _rl_learning_allowed() and _research_influence_allowed(kind, paper_mode=…) (§14.3).

17.3 Drift, staleness and forgetting

  • Feature drift — data_drift_status: DRIFT halves size and is a validator rejection reason (§05.4, §05.5).
  • Model staleness — core/model_staleness_tracker.py with core/meta_adaptive_engine.py; the registry status in §18 carries the operator verdict.
  • Catastrophic forgetting — EWC Fisher penalties plus replay buffers, with research/mars_maml_adapter.py and research/laya_checkpoint_parity.py covering meta-learning and checkpoint parity.
  • Behavioural divergence — research/divergence_monitor.py and operations/degradation_detector.py compare live behaviour to expectations and can demote the autonomy level (§20).
